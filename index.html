A village. A quest. Your choices.

Explore a small RPG built with Dialogue Flow. Talk to different residents, accept Mira’s quest, and see how your choices change their replies.

Welcome to Village Gate

Desktop keyboard and mouse recommended. In-game dialogue is Korean. The sample runs locally in your browser.

Ready to play · WebGL 2 required

Controls

WASD / arrows — walk · E — talk
Click the dialogue / Space — continue
Click a choice / 1–3 — answer · Esc — close

Try the story

Meet residents before accepting a quest. Talk to Mira (미라), then revisit Rowan (로완) and Lina (리나). Find the lantern keeper in the forest, collect the herb, and return to Mira. Use the in-game reset button to try other choices.
